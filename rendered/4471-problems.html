parser: the shared parser reads month first

Ticket: issue 1.

A tenant whose locale writes the day first (asked of the platform, not a list) gets its dates flipped: 7 April comes out as 4 July. Reports reach the desk by app, by email and by form. This is the problem #4471 describes.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first people to type month first

Ticket: issue 2.

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first tenants on that form type what the hint says, and the form then reads it their way. Only form reports from those tenants. The parser fix does not touch this: the parser is right here, the hint is wrong.

one-day: a date moved by exactly one day

Ticket: issue 3.

The desk notes mark these reports as a one-day move. Nothing is swapped: “Tuesday 12 May” arrives as “Monday 11 May”; one client in Lisbon got Tuesday and the same meeting in Toronto got Monday; one invite arrived before the booking was made. Month-and-day swapping cannot produce that. The cause is not established from these reports. A time zone or day boundary problem fits what they describe; engineering has to confirm it.

The desk files these reports under parser or hint by tenant, and counts them there. They are counted again under one-day, because a fix for the swap does not help them.
